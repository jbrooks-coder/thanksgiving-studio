Teacher directions & grade challenges

Give students 2 minutes to name something they appreciate, 15–20 minutes to create, then 3 minutes to share. Choose one activity per lesson. Students may dictate or draw. Thanksgiving traditions differ, and students can choose any helper or fictional character.

K: Paint with two colors, complete “I am thankful for…” aloud, or dictate a short card.
Grade 1: Follow a pixel guide or make a card with one specific thank-you sentence.
Grade 2: Customize pixel art and explain your choices, or write a thank-you with a reason.
Grade 3: Create a symmetrical pixel design or plan a two-scene story with a clear sequence.
Grade 4: Design original pixel art and explain your algorithm, or plan and build a ScratchJr story with a repeat block.

The picture button exports the activity currently selected. For the story planner, it exports both scenes. Students can submit the downloaded PNG through your usual classroom platform. This activity does not collect submissions or send student work anywhere.

Shared devices: Download finished work, then choose New student. Browser storage is local, may be cleared by school settings, and is not a backup. This tool does not open or save ScratchJr project files.
